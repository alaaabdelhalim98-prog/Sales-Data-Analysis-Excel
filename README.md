# 📈 Sales Data Analysis & Insights (Excel)

A comprehensive business intelligence project analyzing retail sales performance using Microsoft Excel.

## 📊 Project Overview
This project processes and analyzes **234 retail sales transactions** (totaling **608,011.85 EGP**) spanning from January to September 2026 across major Egyptian regions.

## 🛠️ Key Files & Sheets Structure
1. **sales_training** (Raw Data): The clean and structured master dataset containing order details, categories, sales reps, and pricing.
2. **Sales_by_Category**: Analyzes revenue distribution across product categories.
   * *Insight:* Office Equipment generates the highest revenue (293K+ EGP), followed closely by Electronics and Accessories.
3. **Sales_Rep_Performance**: Evaluates individual sales representative contributions.
   * *Insight:* Karim Samir leads the team in total revenue, followed closely by Mariam Hassan and Nour Ahmed.
4. **Region_Channel_Analysis**: Breaks down order volumes by geographic region and sales channels.
   * *Insight:* Giza and Cairo record the highest order volume, with Phone and Store channels being primary.
5. **Monthly_Sales_Trend**: Tracks sales momentum over months.
   * *Insight:* Sales peak notably in June, achieving over 110K EGP in monthly revenue.
6. **Product_Discount_Analysis**: Explores product pricing and discount rates.
   * *Insight:* Monitors and Power Banks achieve top sales, with balanced discount rates averaging 4%–5%.

## 🚀 Tools & Techniques
* Microsoft Excel (Advanced Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Cross-Tabulation, Data Formatting, and Key Business Insights).
